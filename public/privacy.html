What we collect

Your name, email, phone number and delivery addresses, your order history, and the messages you send us. When you pay by card we never see or store your full card number: card details are handled by the payment provider.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, take payment, answer support requests, and (only if you subscribe) send newsletters. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in each email.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, sessions use secure cookies, and the site is served over HTTPS in production.

Sharing

We share data only with the payment providers and delivery partners needed to complete your order, or when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

You can update your profile and addresses in your account, and ask us to delete your account by contacting support.
